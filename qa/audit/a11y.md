## Checked, no problem found (summary)
- **Visual rules, every page at 320 to 1440 and at 200% zoom:** radius 0; no shadows, gradients, images or emoji; no motion. The accent appears only on interactive elements. Colors are all tokens apart from the browser defaults above. Icons are Lucide with stroke 2.5, apart from the check icon above. Libre Franklin renders every glyph, in weights 300 to 800 only.
- **Labels, headings and rules:** section labels and headings to spec; the 6px ink rules at the top and above the footer; 1px section rules and hairlines; 3px error borders.
- **Contrast:** every rendered text pair, against what is actually behind it, at 4.71:1 or better.
- **Layout:**
  - the switch at 900px, the 12-column grid, the 1200px container, 40px and 20px side padding
  - the label and content columns, section padding, button sizes and H1 sizes
  - at 1440, the Home and Register geometry matches the artboards to the pixel
- **No horizontal scroll or overflow** at any width or zoom, including Review with long unbroken values at 320.
- **Focus and keyboard:** a 2px ink outline with 2px offset at every stop; no positive tabindex; the menu opens with Enter and closes with Escape; every other tap target is 44px or more.
- **Forms:** every input labelled; real buttons and links only; details and summary for the FAQ; errors linked with aria-invalid and aria-describedby, with focus on the first; status and alert roles on the form results.
- **Board print:** 1 Letter page from both viewports, also with background printing and 0.4in margins; no colored pixels.

## Could not check
- Safari/WebKit and Firefox (placeholder and native control colors, `:has()`, the Safari print dialog), real phones, text-only zoom.
- The interactive print dialog; screen readers; Windows forced colors.
- Chromium computes the 1.5px borders as 1px at device scale 1 and 2, in the site and the artboards alike.
