# Prayer chain and care messages, privacy first

By the end, you can format messages without exposing private details.

**Practice files (all FAKE):** prayer-log.csv, prayer-chain-guide.md.

## The four steps

1. Start a new chat. Paste the guide and the prayer log, and ask for each request with its share level.
2. Ask for the Sunday whole-church email and a permission table.
3. Ask for a short get-well card for Ray Okafor, with [CONFIRM] on anything unsure.
4. Check every line against the prayer log yourself. Then send.

## Prompts to paste

Step 1, with the guide and the log pasted below it:

```
I format our church's prayer chain messages. Below are our prayer chain guide and this week's prayer request log. Do not write any message yet. List each request with its share level, and tell me which ones may go in the whole-church email.
```

Step 2:

```
Write this Sunday's whole-church prayer chain email. Follow the guide's format. Use only rows marked "whole church", and only the words in the request column. Never use the private column. Do not add updates or details that are not in the log. Then add a permission table: one row for every detail in the email, with the log row and share level it came from.
```

Step 3:

```
Now write a get-well card message for Ray Okafor, two or three sentences, signed "Your Maple Street Church family". Use only what he agreed to share with the whole church. If you are not sure a detail was agreed to, leave it out or mark it [CONFIRM] so I can ask.
```

Step 4 is yours. Open the prayer log and tick each detail that matches its row and share level.

## Permission check

- [ ] Every detail comes from the request column of its row.
- [ ] Nothing comes from the private column.
- [ ] The whole-church email has only rows marked whole church.
- [ ] No updates or details that no one wrote down.
- [ ] Pastor-only and do-not-share requests appear nowhere.
- [ ] Every [CONFIRM] is answered by the person before you send.

Practice only with the FAKE files. With real requests, follow your church’s AI use policy (Topic 2), and keep names, health details, children’s details and giving information out of Claude unless that policy allows it.
