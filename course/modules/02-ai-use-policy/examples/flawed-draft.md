# Maple Street Church AI Use Policy

Adopted by the board, March 10.

**Why we have this policy.** Staff already use AI tools for office work. This policy keeps that work accurate and keeps member information private.

**What staff may use AI for.** Drafts of newsletters, announcements, schedules, reminders and minutes.

**A person checks everything.** A staff member reads and checks every draft before it is sent, printed or posted.

**What never goes in.** Prayer requests, health details, children’s information and giving records never go into an AI tool with a member’s name attached. State law requires written consent before any member information is entered into an AI tool.

**Accounts.** Use only an account set up with a church email address, never a personal account.

**New tools.** Pastor Dan Hale approves any new AI tool before staff use it for church work.

**Telling the congregation.** Anything written with help from AI must say so at the bottom.

**Review.** This policy is reviewed by the board each January.
