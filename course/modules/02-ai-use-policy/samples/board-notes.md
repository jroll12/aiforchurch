# Board meeting notes, Tuesday, March 10

Maple Street Church (FAKE), Riverton (FAKE). Present: Ellen Marsh (chair), Pastor Dan Hale, Carol Lin, Tom Becker, Ben Adler (finance). Notes by Rita Gomez.

## Item 3: Staff use of AI tools

Pastor Dan shared the staff survey. Four of six staff already use AI tools for some office work. He asked the board for a short written policy.

Concerns raised:
- Carol Lin, on member privacy: “What does the law require before we put member information into an AI tool?” No one knew. No answer was recorded.
- Tom Becker: “Does our denomination have a rule on this?” No one knew.
- Ellen Marsh: mistakes going out under the church’s name.
- Ben Adler: giving records must stay private.

Decided (vote 5 to 0):
1. Staff may use AI tools to draft office work: newsletters, announcements, schedules, reminders and minutes.
2. A staff member reads and checks every draft before it is sent, printed or posted.
3. Do not put prayer requests, health details, children’s information or giving records into an AI tool with a member’s name attached.
4. Staff use AI tools only with an account set up with a church email address, not a personal account.
5. Pastor Dan approves any new AI tool before staff use it for church work.
6. Review this policy in six months, at the September meeting.

Discussed, not decided:
- Whether to tell the congregation when something was written with help from AI. Tabled to the April meeting.

Next step: Rita brings a one-page draft policy to the April meeting for the board to review.
