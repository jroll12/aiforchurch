# A church AI use policy

By the end, you can write a one-page policy your pastor and board can review.

**Practice files (all FAKE):** board-notes.md, staff-survey.csv, current-computer-policy.md.

**What you take home is a draft.** Your pastor and board decide what it becomes.

## The four steps

1. Start a new chat. Paste the three files and ask what was decided and what was left open.
2. Ask for a one-page draft policy and a rules table.
3. Tell Claude to mark [CONFIRM] any rule with no source, and anything about the law or your denomination.
4. Read the rules table against the board notes yourself. Then give the draft to your pastor and board.

## Prompts to paste

Step 1, with the three files pasted below it:

```
I am drafting an AI use policy for our church office. Below are our board meeting notes, a short staff survey and our current computer and email use policy. Do not write the policy yet. First list three things: what the board decided, what it discussed but did not decide, and what questions were left without an answer.
```

Step 2:

```
Write a one-page draft AI use policy for our staff, in plain words. Put "Draft for board review" at the top. Use only what the board decided and what our current policy says. Then add a rules table: one row for every rule in the draft, with the exact line from the notes or the current policy it came from.
```

Step 3:

```
Check the draft again. If a rule has no source line, do not keep it as a rule. If anything says what the law or our denomination requires, do not state it as fact. Mark each of these [CONFIRM] and list them under the table, with who should answer each one: the pastor, the board or a lawyer.
```

Step 4 is yours. Read each row of the rules table against the board notes and tick it.

## Decision-line check

- [ ] Every rule has a source line: a board decision or the current policy.
- [ ] Nothing discussed but not decided became a rule.
- [ ] Anything about the law or the denomination is marked [CONFIRM] for the pastor, the board or a lawyer.
- [ ] The rules table matches the board notes, read line by line.
- [ ] The top says “Draft for board review.”

Practice with the FAKE files only. For your own policy, paste board decisions and your current policy, and leave out member names, prayer requests, health details, children’s details and giving information.
