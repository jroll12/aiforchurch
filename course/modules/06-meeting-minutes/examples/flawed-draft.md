# Maple Street Church (FAKE) Board: Minutes of Tuesday, April 7, 2026

**Present:** Carol Bennett (chair), Pastor Dan Hale, Tom Reyes, Linda Shaw (treasurer), Ruth Adams, Mark Ellis (secretary).
**Also present:** Rita Gomez (office manager).
**Absent:** Greg Moore.

**Call to order.** Carol Bennett called the meeting to order at 7:05 p.m. in Room 2. Pastor Dan Hale opened with prayer.

**March minutes.** Moved by Ruth Adams, seconded by Linda Shaw, to approve the March minutes as written. Motion carried, all in favor.

**Treasurer’s report.** Linda Shaw presented the March report. Filed for review.

**Roof repair.** Tom Reyes presented two quotes for the leak over Fellowship Hall: Hillside Roofing (FAKE), $9,500, with a 5-year warranty, and Summit Roof Co. **Next meeting.** In May. Carol Bennett will send the date.

**Adjourned** at 8:20 p.m. Ruth Adams closed with prayer.
