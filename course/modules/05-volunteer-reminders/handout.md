# Volunteer schedules and reminder emails [CONFIRM]

By the end, you can draft a month of reminders from a sign-up sheet.

**Practice files (all FAKE):** reminder-guide.md, signup-sheet.csv, swap-email.md.

## The four steps

1. Open a new chat. Paste the reminder guide, the April sign-up sheet and the swap email.
2. Ask for a reminder list and a short personal email for each filled slot.
3. Tell Claude to apply the swap, and to leave the empty slot empty, marked [CONFIRM].
4. Check every row of the list against the sign-up sheet and the swap email, name by name.

## Prompts to paste

Step 1, with the guide, the sheet and the email pasted below it:

```
You help write volunteer reminder emails for our church. Below are our reminder guide, the volunteer sign-up sheet for April, and a later email about a swap. Every name, date, role and email address must come from the sheet or the email. Never add a name that is not there.
```

Step 2:

```
Make a reminder list: one row for every Sunday and role on the sheet, with the name, the email address and the date to send the reminder. Then write a short personal reminder email for each row that has a name, following the guide.
```

Step 3:

```
Check the list against the sheet and the swap email again. Apply any swap from the email to both Sundays. If a slot on the sheet has no name, do not fill it in and do not write an email for it. Mark it [CONFIRM] to recruit someone, and list it under the table.
```

Step 4 is yours. Open the sheet and the email, and tick each row of the reminder list that matches.

## Name-to-slot check

- [ ] Every reminder has a matching row on the sheet: same name, date and role.
- [ ] Every swap from a later email is applied to both Sundays.
- [ ] Every email address belongs to the person named.
- [ ] Empty slots have no reminder and are marked [CONFIRM] to recruit someone.
- [ ] No children’s names anywhere.

Practice with the FAKE sheet only, and bring no real names or email addresses. Keep children’s names and family details out of reminders, including nursery reminders.
